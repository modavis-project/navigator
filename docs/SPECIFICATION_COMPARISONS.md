# Comparing specification evidence

On an organ’s Specification page, **Compare all accounts** displays technical
assertions and captured coverage side by side. Each column retains a source
account, its dating qualification and exact revision. Open a value’s evidence
for the source record, original wording and field locator. A comparison action
beside a differing value opens the corresponding field.

Multiple claims from one source record remain separate. Counts distinguish
assertions, value groups, source records and source accounts. A majority of
matching claims does not select an authoritative value. Numeric comparison uses
eligible normalized numbers; qualified quantities and literal wording remain
separate. Missing information is neither a zero nor a disagreement.

Some related descriptions use different scopes. General transmission descriptions
and specific key-action or stop-action claims can be reviewed together under
**Action descriptions need comparison**. Their original field labels remain
visible. This review does not automatically equate scopes, translate a source
claim into a new assertion, or decide whether the claims contradict one another.

**Compare stoplists** retains two complete source lists with their original
divisions and wording. Highlighting compares occurrences of the same recorded
name and pitch, including duplicate multiplicity. It does not establish physical
component identity. An account without a captured stoplist cannot supply negative
evidence that another account’s stops were absent.

**No captured stop rows** describes stored coverage. A source may still provide
couplers, accessories, technical facts or a prose summary. This state does not
mean the organ has zero stops, nor establish whether a missing list reflects the
original page or an incomplete extraction. An explicit source-reported zero is
retained as a separate assertion. Reported totals and captured list-row counts
also remain separate when they differ.

Historical, proposed and earlier-instrument accounts keep their own scope. Main
account facts are not inherited by another dated description. Matching values,
differing values and source observation dates do not establish a shared physical
configuration, a physical change, continuous validity or present condition.
