# Reading the evidence

A source account is evidence about an instrument at a particular or unknown time.
It is not automatically its present configuration. Separate descriptions retain
their divisions, stops, totals, dates and source attribution.

Stop and division searches operate on recorded wording. Exact matching ignores
case and repeated spaces but retains modifiers and pitch-bearing names. Broader
wording matching can recover those variants while also returning different names.
A lexical match does not establish equivalent stops or a shared physical object.

Occurrence, specification-account, event-record and organ counts have different
denominators. Coverage charts report documented evidence, not the prevalence of
features in the world's organs. Multiple records from one provider do not provide
independent corroboration.

Actor links distinguish canonical identities from unresolved source assertions.
Network edges summarize the stated evidence relation; shared work on an organ
does not itself establish apprenticeship, collaboration or influence.

Maps retain coordinate precision and historical qualification. Relocation
connectors join documented endpoints without reconstructing a travel route.
OMARO remains an independently versioned reference, distinct from POD physical
instruments and unreviewed source terminology.

Use record citations and evidence downloads with the selected dataset snapshot.
The compact FAQs and method panels explain the relevant limits beside each
research workflow. A structurally valid semantic export does not independently
establish historical truth.
